# Batalla de Matasiete

An arcade defense shooter: a lone patriota holds the mountainside of Margarita against the 1817 royalist reconquest.

## Language

**Patriota**:
The soldier the player controls — the last defender of Margarita.
_Avoid_: player, protagonista, héroe

**Español**:
The royalist soldiers attacking the island; every enemy on screen.
_Avoid_: realista, enemigo, invasor

**Isla**:
Margarita Island, the land the patriota defends.
_Avoid_: Venezuela, territorio

**Sable**:
The patriota's melee weapon — a close-range sweep.
_Avoid_: espada, machete

**Piedra**:
The patriota's special: a score-charged boulder and the gauge that holds its charge.
_Avoid_: roca, carga, bomba

**Lancero**:
A slow foot soldier who advances directly at the patriota.
_Avoid_: grunt, infante, soldado básico

**Jinete**:
A cavalryman on horseback; the fastest melee español.
_Avoid_: caballero, jinete de otra montura

**Fusilero**:
An español who stops at mid-range and fires his musket.
_Avoid_: tirador, shooter, arquero

**Oleada**:
A single wave of españoles attacking together.
_Avoid_: wave, ronda, nivel
